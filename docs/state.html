How this is calculated
The early-vote sections below show who has voted, not how they voted. Party here is voter registration. Unaffiliated voters are close to a third of ballots, and early voting shifts from cycle to cycle. Treat any “lead” in these sections as an assumption, not a measurement.

Why to be careful

  • Cannibalization. Most early voters are reliable voters who would have voted anyway. A party “banking” more early votes may just have moved its Election Day voters earlier.
  • Habits shift. Republicans leaned toward Election Day voting in 2020 and 2022, then pushed early voting in 2024. A change in the gap versus 2022 can reflect that shift rather than a change in support.
  • Registration isn’t vote choice. Older registered Democrats in rural counties often vote Republican, and unaffiliated voters split differently every year.
  • Rebuilt baselines. 2018 and 2022 curves come from the final files’ return dates, which can differ slightly from what was published on each day at the time.
