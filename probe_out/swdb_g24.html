2024 General Election Precinct Data

The message "no data yet" indicates that the file is still being collected and processed. It will be available at a later date. The message "unavailable" indicates that the data are not available from the county.

Data Notes:

  • Vote counts in Madera County files posted here do not match the certified vote count. Madera County has not yet provided Statewide Database with legally required data necessary to produce accurate redistricting data. While we work to resolve this situation, we have published partial data for Madera County on this page.
  • SOV Local files include electoral contests at the County, City, School District, Community College District, and Special District levels. Contest and candidate information is included in the data file, so a separate codebook is not required. State level SOVs may be posted before Local SOVs.
  • Small precincts with fewer than ten voters are masked for privacy. Contest specific vote counts are not reported for these precincts. In the csv's, all values other than total vote and party split totals are "asterisked" out. Those fields in the dbfs are integers so they are zeroed out instead.
  • 2024 General Election precinct files currently include artifacts that will be removed in an update. These artifacts are a temporary side effect of methodology improvements implemented this cycle.
